🎮 How to Play

1. Select your chip value from the chip selector
2. Click on the betting table to place your bets
3. Use Quick Bets for faster betting
4. Click "SPIN" to start the roulette wheel
5. Watch the wheel spin and see if you win!
6. Your balance is automatically saved

💡 Tips

• You can place multiple bets before spinning
• Click the same spot multiple times to stack chips
• Use "Clear Bets" to remove all placed bets
• Your balance resets to $200 if you hit $0
• Switch between European and American wheels
